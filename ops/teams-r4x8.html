    Quick guide

    Onboard a new agency
    1. New agency below: name, plan, seats, contact + billing email.
    2. Open it → Dashboard access: invite the chief / wellness lead (they set their own password).
    3. Send the join code to the agency — members enter it in the app (Profile → Join your agency).
    4. In Roster, mark the peer supporters and set stations.
    5. Billing: paid plans stay off until paid — their admin pays by card on the dashboard, or you send an annual invoice here (or activate a trusted PO agency early). Pilots start right away.
    Plans and prices
    Essentials $4.99 / person / month ($59.88 / seat / year) — peer support program, no Pro.
    Teams Pro $7.99 / person / month ($95.88 / seat / year) — Essentials + AfterCall Pro on every seat.
    Enterprise — custom price per agency, invoiced (plan id command, grants Pro).
    Pilot — Teams Pro for a set number of days, invoiced once.
    At least 10 seats are billed. Discounts are per-agency contract terms.

    Send an invoice

    For agencies paying by PO, check or bank transfer. Stripe emails it to the billing email, due in 30 days. Card-paying agencies don't need this — their admin pays from the Teams dashboard.

    Not active yet — waiting for the first payment

    Members can't join and the agency's dashboard only shows billing until they pay. For a PO agency you trust, you can let them in now — remember to collect payment.

    Paid outside Stripe

    Agency pays you directly (no Stripe at all)? Mark billing active so it shows as paid here.
