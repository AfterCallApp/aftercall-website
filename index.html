Privacy & PHI

Built to keep PHI out.
From the first tap.

AfterCall is a personal journal, not a chart. It's not an ePCR or a patient record, and it never writes into one.

PHI screen on your phone.

Potential patient information is flagged and blocked on your phone before anything is saved. Every call you share is screened by AI and verified, every time.

Voice and CAD cleaned first.

Names, addresses, dates and incident numbers are removed. The original audio and CAD text are never saved.

Your reflections are yours.

Your agency never sees your journal, check-ins, messages or peer support conversations.

Never used against you.

Nothing in AfterCall can be used in discipline, promotion or fitness-for-duty decisions.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

For providers

Is it really free?

Yes. Call logging, journaling, check-ins, CE tracking and Communities are free, always, plus 3 free AfterCall AI narratives. Pro adds unlimited AfterCall AI and advanced tools.

Can my agency see what I write?

No. Your journal, check-ins, messages and peer support conversations are private to you.

Does it replace my ePCR?

No. Your PCR stays the legal record. AfterCall is your personal log and wellness tool.

Is patient information safe?

PHI is screened and blocked on your phone before anything is saved. Voice and CAD text are cleaned first, and the originals are never stored.

What happens to my data if I leave my agency?

It's yours. You keep your entire history: calls, journals, shifts and badges. The seat ends; your account stays.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. Try it free for 7 days and cancel anytime.

For agencies

Does AfterCall replace our peer support team or EAP?

No. It makes them easier to reach. Your peer supporters, crisis line and EAP live right inside the app.

What does leadership actually see?

Anonymous team trends only. Groups under five are hidden automatically. You never see the person, the journal or the conversation.

Does it integrate with our ePCR?

No. AfterCall doesn't write into ESO, ImageTrend or Zoll, and no patient identifier is retained.

What does it cost?

Teams Essentials is $4.99 per seat per month and Teams Pro is $7.99 per seat per month.

Can we use grant money?

Many agencies can. Wellness programs qualify under FEMA AFG and several state and private grants.

Can AfterCall be used in discipline?

Never. Nothing in AfterCall may be used in discipline, promotion, assignment or fitness-for-duty decisions, and we'll put that in writing alongside your labor group.
