Fatigue

weekly average · Samn-Perelli 1–7
The scale used in aviation and EMS fatigue research: 1 fully alert · 4 a little tired · 5 moderately tired · 7 completely exhausted. The shaded band (5–7) is the level fatigue research treats as a safety risk. Asked on post-call and daily check-ins (app 1.3.0 and later).

How suppression protects your people

Every wellbeing number on this page is an aggregate over at least 5 contributing members — per window, per week, and per station. Below that floor the number is withheld entirely, so a small group can never be reverse-read. Adoption runs on trust; this is what keeps it.

The alert loop

every peer alert in the window
A flag opens when a member marks a call high-impact, has a heavy run (4+ Serious or Critical calls, or 3+ Critical, within 24 hours), or taps “Ask for a check-in”. Your peer supporters get a signal-only ping — never the call, never the content. It is picked up when a supporter reaches out, and followed up when that conversation is done. Peer alert rules

Besides a member marking a call high-impact or asking for a check-in, AfterCall can alert your peer supporters automatically when one member has a heavy run of high-acuity calls. Set what a heavy run means for your agency. Supporters learn only that someone had a heavy stretch — never the calls, never anything they wrote — and you only ever see the count.

The agreement, in one card

Wellness reporting is aggregate-only on every plan · groups under five are suppressed · alerts carry the signal, never the content · a member who leaves keeps their own history · members can leave the workspace at any time · nothing in AfterCall may be used in discipline, promotion, assignment, or fitness-for-duty.
