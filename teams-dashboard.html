How a flag works: when a member marks a call high-impact, a flag opens and your peer supporters get a signal-only ping — never the call, never the content. It becomes acknowledged when a supporter picks it up and reaches out, and closed when the follow-up is done. These three numbers are the health of that loop: if the median creeps up or flags sit open past a week, the peer team needs more hands.

How suppression protects your people

Every wellbeing number on this page is an aggregate over at least 5 contributing members — per window, per week, and per station. Below that floor the number is withheld entirely, so a small group can never be reverse-read. Adoption runs on trust; this is what keeps it.

The agreement, in one card

Wellness reporting is aggregate-only on every plan · groups under five are suppressed · alerts carry the signal, never the content · a member who leaves keeps their own history · members can leave the workspace at any time · nothing in AfterCall may be used in discipline, promotion, assignment, or fitness-for-duty.
