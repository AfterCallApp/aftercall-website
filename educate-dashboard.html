Downloads

CSV files that open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
Downloads include students who are enrolled now. When a student graduates or leaves, their records are no longer shown here, so download what you need for your files first.
Progress summary

One row per student: hours, patient contacts and team leads against your requirements, and how many shifts are verified, waiting or sent back.

Shift log

One row per shift record: date, hours, site, status, who signed it, whether they are on your preceptor list, and who verified it.

Patient contact log

One row per patient contact with everything the student documented: age range, call type, role, vitals, narrative fields and skills. No patient names.

A single shift record

Open any shift record from Sign-offs or a student's page and choose Print to save it as a PDF with the preceptor's signature.
